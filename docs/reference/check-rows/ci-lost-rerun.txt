watches: **a CI job the infrastructure lost: GitHub cancelled it because no runner acquired it (harbor#675), or the runner lost its connection mid-job (harbor#1060, 2026-10-06 run 37511778240)** (operator decision 2026-10-06), by `bin/ci-runners.sh rerun-lost` on the repos `ci.runners` already covers (the fleet rows on `CI_RUNS_ON=self-hosted` with their runner online, an org's repos while its runner is online) plus `VytCepas/harbor` while `harbor-linux` is online: a run of the last 48h, still on attempt 1, with a failed or cancelled job that has **no step concluded `failure`** and is one of three shapes: (1) no steps and check-run annotations ALL `was not acquired by Runner` (read by `bin/ci-lost-job.sh`, the one reader); (2) a job that FAILED (never `cancelled`) with a step with no conclusion (`conclusion: null`, the runner dropped mid-step) and no annotation; (3) annotations ALL the runner's own `lost communication with the server` or `received a shutdown signal` (`ci-lost-job.sh --runner-pattern`). A job with a failed step, a cancelled job with only an open step (a human cancel, a timeout, a fail-fast sibling), or carrying any other annotation (and `The operation was canceled.`), is never touched. It re-runs that one job (`gh run rerun <run> --job <id>`, never `--failed`, which would re-run a real failure beside it), **once**: `cache/ci-lost-reruns.tsv` is written before the call, keyed by job id, and a re-run run is on attempt 2 so is not looked at again. A second lost job in the same run is left to a person (`pr-wait` prints its command). **API per tick**: 1 run list per repo, then per NEW failed or cancelled run at most 20 per tick (`HARBOR_CI_LOST_READS`) 1 jobs read plus 1 annotation read per failed or cancelled job that has no failed step; a run with no lost job is remembered in `cache/ci-lost-seen.tsv` and not read again
schedule: every 300s, inside `status.fleet-snapshot`'s run, at the end of `ci-runners.sh tick`; falsifier: `sh tests/ci/runners/lost.test.sh` (stubbed `gh`: a lost-only job re-run once, the second tick nothing, a job with steps, a mixed-annotation job, a billing refusal, a run past 48h or past attempt 1, a refused re-run retried, the off switch, an offline runner; the open-step, lost-communication and shutdown-signal shapes re-run once each; a real test failure, a failed step beside an open one, a cancel, a human cancel or a timeout or a fail-fast sibling with an open step, and a second infrastructure failure of the same job never; and the same suite goes red when the reader takes ANY annotation instead of ALL, drops the failed-step filter, or drops the attempt filter)
