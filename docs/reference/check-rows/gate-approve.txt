watches: **the merge and deploy approval gate** (harbor#1018), by the installed `<cfg>/bin/gate-approve.sh` (`merge <repo> <pr> <sha>` or `deploy <repo> <sha>`): the operator's click on Claude Code's own permission dialog (a `permissions.ask` rule naming that exact installed text). A merge is bound to the PR and the head sha clicked and runs only on green CI at that head (through merge-stack, which pins `--match-head-commit`); a deploy is bound to the default-branch sha. Red is refused even when approved; the floor guard refuses every other spelling (`bash:gate-noncanonical`) and any call where no human can answer the prompt (`bash:gate-nohuman`, from `floor_no_human`); the tool itself refuses when no ask rule is installed, a PermissionRequest hook or approving mod exists, or its parent `claude` was started with `-p`, `--settings` or a permission-skipping flag. [docs/design/approval-gate.md](docs/design/approval-gate.md)
schedule: **not scheduled**: on demand, run only when a click launches it. Falsifier: `sh tests/gate/approval/merge.test.sh` (a stale head, another PR, red, and one ledger row per answer, each with a mutant), `ancestry.test.sh`, `queue.test.sh`, `deploy.test.sh`, and `sh tests/admission/bash/gate.test.sh` (the spellings and the no-human rows). No test covers a real operator click
notifies: one row per answer, success or refusal, in `~/.claude/.helm-approvals/ledger.tsv` (time, action, args, sha256 of args, result); a refused protected merge also queues a row the statusline shows as `⧗N`
red: an approval was refused: `refused-stale` (the head or main moved since the click), `refused-red` (CI not green), `refused-moved`, `merge-failed` or `runner-failed rc=N`. Nothing was merged or deployed on a refusal. Read the last ledger row, then ask again for the printed command. Disable: there is no switch that approves. `touch ~/.claude/.harbor-autoship.off` stops every unattended merge and deploy and leaves the click working; to retire the gate, remove its `permissions.ask` rule for the installed `gate-approve.sh` from `settings.json` (the tool then refuses every call, fail-closed) and delete this row
