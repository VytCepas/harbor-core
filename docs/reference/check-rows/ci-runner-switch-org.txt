watches: **an organisation's own runner verdict** (harbor#1060), by `bin/ci-runner-switch.sh --org ORG` for each `org ORG [cap]` line in `~/.claude/.harbor-ci-runners`: the org's month-to-date Actions minutes (billing `usage` at `orgs/ORG`, product `actions`, sku Linux, macOS or Windows, every repo, a deleted one's too) against the cap on that line, default 2,000 (operator decision 2026-10-05 for the box's org). At or over the cap the verdict is `self-hosted`, under it and on the 1st (UTC, without reading the bill) `github-hosted`. **It sets no variable**: `cache/runner-switch.<ORG>.json` is the verdict, and `ci.runners` sets the org-level `CI_RUNS_ON` only while the org's runner is online. Decided apart from the personal account's `ci.runner-switch` verdict
schedule: every 300s, inside `status.fleet-snapshot`'s run, after `ci.runner-switch`; falsifier: the `--org` section of `sh tests/ci/runner-switch/runner-switch.test.sh` (stubbed `gh`: under, at, the cap from config, the 1st, a failed read, controls)
notifies: **One `[check:ci.runner-switch-org]` line in `~/.claude/cache/checks.log` per change of verdict** (`ORG github-hosted -> self-hosted (2000/2000 minutes, at or over the cap)`), and one on the way into a failed read; never one per tick. No desktop notification, no statusline token. Detail by hand: `sh bin/ci-runner-switch.sh --org ORG --print` (writes nothing)
red: the org has spent its cap of hosted minutes this month and its CI moves to the org runner on this box (`ci.runners` flips the org's `CI_RUNS_ON`). A fallback, not a fault: wait for the 1st, or raise the number on the `org` line. **A failed read keeps the last verdict, marked unread, and `ci.runners` trusts no unread verdict**: `gh auth status` (the org's bill needs `admin:org`). Disable: delete the `org` line from `~/.claude/.harbor-ci-runners` (no verdict, so `ci.runners` leaves the org as it is), delete `~/.claude/cache/runner-switch.<ORG>.json`, and put the org back on hosted with `gh variable delete CI_RUNS_ON --org <ORG>`
