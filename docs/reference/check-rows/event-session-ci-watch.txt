watches: **a PR's checks, watched until they settle** (harbor#1044): the preset that runs `session-ci-watch.sh` on `pr.opened`; it starts `bin/ci-wait.sh` detached in rounds of `HARBOR_CI_WATCH_SECS` (1800 s), re-armed while checks are still running up to `HARBOR_CI_WATCH_CEILING` (7200 s), and never merges, approves or re-runs anything
schedule: **not scheduled: a rule of `event.dispatch`, on by deploy** (`#default` in `contracts/event-rules.tsv`; `event-dispatch.sh reconcile` installs it, harbor#1437). It fires on `pr.opened`, debounced 30 s, at most 12 an hour. On: `harbor event on --preset event.session-ci-watch`. Off: `harbor event on --off event.session-ci-watch`; falsifier: `sh tests/session/react/presets.test.sh`
notifies: one still-running notice after the first round that ends pending, then the verdict (green, red, or stuck past the ceiling), each naming any failed, cancelled or pending check, each as a desktop banner, a `reactions.log` line and a note in `~/.local/state/harbor/session-notes/<session>` that `context.ci-verdict-for-session` prints on the opening session's next Bash, Read, Grep or Glob call (2026-11-16: a relay job cancelled at 1 h read only "still running after 1800s", and the session never saw it)
red: checks red, or a watch stuck past its ceiling. Disable: `harbor event on --off event.session-ci-watch`; a watch already started ends at its own ceiling; the session note alone: delete `context-rules/user/ci-verdict-for-session.md`, then `harbor inject --live --apply`
