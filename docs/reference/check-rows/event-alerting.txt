watches: **the pulse, evaluated when something it watches moves** (harbor#1044; the old id `event.pulse` is read as this one until 2026-11-15, harbor#961): the preset in `contracts/event-rules.tsv` that runs `alert-notify.sh` on an event rather than on `alert.probe`'s 900 s timer
schedule: **not scheduled: a rule of `event.dispatch`, on by deploy** (`#default` in `contracts/event-rules.tsv`; `event-dispatch.sh reconcile` installs it, harbor#1437). It fires on `ci.job.done`, `obligation.open`, `obligation.close` or `git.merge`, debounced 300 s per subject, at most 4 an hour, and on a `rule.backstop` after 3600 s without one. On: `harbor event on --preset event.alerting`, then the operator may boot out `com.harbor.alert.probe`. Off: `harbor event on --off event.alerting`; falsifier: `sh tests/event/dispatch/registry.test.sh`
notifies: as `alert.probe`: desktop, on a change only; a non-zero exit is a `[check:event.alerting]` line in `cache/checks.log`
red: as `alert.probe`. Disable: `harbor event on --off event.alerting`, and put `com.harbor.alert.probe` back in launchd if its timer was booted out
