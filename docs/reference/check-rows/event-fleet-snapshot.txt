watches: **the fleet snapshot, refreshed when the fleet moves** (harbor#1044): the preset in `contracts/event-rules.tsv` that runs `status-fleet-snapshot-refresh.sh` on an event rather than on `status.fleet-snapshot`'s 300 s timer
schedule: **not scheduled: a rule of `event.dispatch`, on by deploy** (`#default` in `contracts/event-rules.tsv`; `event-dispatch.sh reconcile` installs it, harbor#1437). It fires on `session.start`, `session.stop`, `pr.opened`, `pr.merged`, `git.merge` or `ci.job.done`, debounced 60 s per subject, at most 12 an hour, and on a `rule.backstop` after 3600 s without one. On: `harbor event on --preset event.fleet-snapshot`, then the operator may boot out `com.harbor.status.fleet-snapshot`. Off: `harbor event on --off event.fleet-snapshot`; falsifier: `sh tests/event/dispatch/registry.test.sh`
notifies: as `status.fleet-snapshot`, the cache only; a non-zero exit is a `[check:event.fleet-snapshot]` line in `cache/checks.log`
red: as `status.fleet-snapshot`: a stale glyph. Disable: `harbor event on --off event.fleet-snapshot`, and put `com.harbor.status.fleet-snapshot` back in launchd if its timer was booted out (docs/reference/checks.md, Platforms)
