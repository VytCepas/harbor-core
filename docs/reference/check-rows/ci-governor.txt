watches: **how many CPU cores CI may hold on this Mac, and what to do when it spikes** (harbor#1032, operator requests 2026-10-04), by `bin/ci-governor.sh`: a budget of 4 cores while the user is active and 8 after 600s idle (`ioreg` HIDIdleTime, read when something wakes it), halved when the 1-min load passes 8 (never below 2, again at most every 60s), raised 2 at a time no sooner than 120s after the last change and only under a load of 6. Applied by `docker update --cpus` on every Linux runner container (live, capped at the Colima VM's CPUs): `linux_runners` if listed, else discovered each tick by one `docker ps` (every running `*-linux` container on the actions-runner image). **Priority classes (harbor#1060):** `low_priority` (default `harbor-linux,fleet-linux,yard-linux`: harbor, Fleet, Yard) yields to every other repo's container: a busy normal container gets an even share of the budget less `linux_min` 1 per busy low one, busy low ones split what remains (never under `linux_min`), an idle one sits at `linux_min`; a normal job starting cuts a busy low one on that tick and its end restores it, **except that a busy low job older than `lift_after` 900s (half of harbor's 30-minute job timeout; the worker's own elapsed time, read by the same `docker exec` that answers busy; 0 = off) keeps `lift_cpus` 2, capped so every other busy container keeps `linux_min`** (harbor#1060: `suites` timed out twice at load 20+ at the floor; design and the operator-side options in `docs/design/ci-local-heal.md`). A managed Mac runner (`mac_runners`, none by default) is stopped when the budget leaves no room for a job, it is idle both here and on GitHub and no macOS job is queued there (a queued job keeps it up, or starts it if the governor parked it: liveness beats the budget; an unreadable answer parks and says so), started again only if the governor stopped it. Other runners' jobs (a runner of another repo) are counted, never managed. Local suite runs take a slot each through `ci-governor.sh run` (`hooks/pre-push`, `tests/lib/run.sh`, `bin/test-suite-run.sh`): budget minus the cores CI holds, at least 1. **The emergency ladder** for a spike the load average shows 30-90s late: a CPU busy at or above `cpu_emergency` 95% over iostat's 1s sample on `spike_samples` 2 samples in a row while the user is active, memory pressure at or above `mem_emergency` 4 (`kern.memorystatus_vm_pressure_level`), or a `pmset -g therm` warning level or CPU speed limit under `thermal_speed_min` 90% on one. Then (a) the budget to `min_budget` at once, (b) SIGSTOP every process under a local `run` slot (SIGCONT after; at most `stop_max` 300s), (c) every Linux runner to `linux_emergency_cpus` 0.5, (d) a busy managed Mac job's Runner.Worker tree to darwin background with `taskpolicy -b -p` (`-B` after). Recovery needs `clear_secs` 60 with no hot sample, then the slow raise. Every default is a key in `~/.claude/.harbor-ci-governor`
schedule: **on events, never a timer: launchd `com.harbor.ci.governor` keeps `ci-governor.sh listen` alive**, blocked on `notifyutil -w` (com.harbor.ci-governor.event from the host runners' job hook `bin/ci-governor-hook.sh` and from every `run` slot taken or given back; the OS keys com.apple.system.thermalpressurelevel, com.apple.system.memorystatus, kern.memorystatus_level) and on `tail -f` of `~/runners/harbor-linux/events/trigger`, which every runner container's job hook appends to (each mounts that dir at `/gov-events`). Each wake is one decision. The emergency sampler (every `sample_interval` 2s plus iostat's 1s, a tick every `tick_interval` 30s) runs only while CI or a slot holds cores or an emergency is on, then exits: a box holding nothing is never polled, and a spike from an unrelated process on such a box is not seen. ProcessType Standard, never Background; falsifier: `sh tests/ci/governor/governor.test.sh`, `sh tests/ci/governor/priority.test.sh` (the lift: 899s cut and 900s kept, the floors, `lift_after=0`, an unread age, the emergency) and the `CI⇣` cases of `sh tests/status/statusline/row2.test.sh`
notifies: **One `[check:ci.governor]` line in `~/.claude/cache/checks.log` per CHANGE** of budget or runner state (`budget 8 -> 4 (load 9.10 over 8); harbor-linux --cpus 8 -> 4`), never one per event; a failing action is said once, on the way in. An emergency is one line in (`EMERGENCY: memory pressure level 4; ...`) and one out (`emergency over (clear 60s)`). The statusline token `CI⇣4/8` while CI is held under its target, the alarm `CI EMERG` during an emergency, `gov?` when the listener recorded in `~/.claude/cache/ci-governor.json` is not running. No desktop notification. Detail by hand: `sh bin/ci-governor.sh status`; what a decision would do: `sh bin/ci-governor.sh tick --dry-run`
red: information, not an alarm: `CI⇣` means CI is throttled because the box is busy or the user is at it, and jobs run slower or queue (a stopped Mac runner takes none). A `WARN` line is red: an action failed (`docker update`, `svc.sh`); `status` names the runner. `gov?` means nothing is deciding, and a `run` whose state is stale decides for itself. **It never kills a job**: a cap only slows a running one, and a stop needs no `Runner.Worker` here AND `busy=false` from GitHub AND no queued macOS job (an unanswered busy ask stops nothing). `CI EMERG` means local runs are paused and CI is at its floor until 60s after the spike; a sample over 60s old counts as clear, so a dead sampler cannot hold one. `docker pause` is never used: a paused runner sends GitHub no heartbeat and how long GitHub waits is unmeasured. Disable: `launchctl bootout gui/$(id -u)/com.harbor.ci.governor` (its SIGTERM makes `listen` continue whatever it stopped first; `sh ~/.claude/bin/ci-governor.sh resume` does it by hand) and `rm ~/Library/LaunchAgents/com.harbor.ci.governor.plist`; remove the two `ACTIONS_RUNNER_HOOK_JOB_*` lines from a host runner's `~/runners/<name>/.env` and each container's env; then give the caps back (`docker update --cpus "$(docker info --format '{{.NCPU}}')" <container>` for each `*-linux` runner, the whole VM) and start a runner it parked (`cd ~/runners/<name> && ./svc.sh start`); `rm -rf ~/.claude/cache/ci-governor ~/.claude/cache/ci-governor.json` makes every `run` a plain run and the token disappear
