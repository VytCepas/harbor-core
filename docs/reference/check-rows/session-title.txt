watches: **the model's session title, applied** (harbor#1257): the hook `hooks/session-title/session-title.sh` reads `<config dir>/session-titles/<session_id>.txt` (one line `<area>: <what>`, at most 50 characters, written by the model with a plain Write) and emits it as `sessionTitle` on UserPromptSubmit and SessionStart (startup, resume, fork)
schedule: **not scheduled.** It runs at every UserPromptSubmit and those SessionStart sources, wired by `runtime-install`; falsifier: `sh hooks/session-title/session-title.test.sh` (mutation: removing the length cap, the operator-title guard or the session-id keying each reddens it)
notifies: `hookSpecificOutput.sessionTitle`; over 50 characters a `systemMessage` naming the limit and no title. Nothing otherwise. Exit 0 on every path
red: a title that never appears in `/resume`, or one that replaced an operator `/rename` (the hook stays silent when `session_title` is non-empty and differs). Disable: `touch ~/.claude/session-title.off` (`runtime-install` then stops wiring it)
